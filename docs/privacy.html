Pickleball Pools privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026 · User guide

Pickleball Pools is a free, open-source app that the Heart of Wisconsin Pickleball Association (HOWPA) uses to run pickleball tournaments. This page explains what information it handles, why, where it is kept, and how to remove it. The app has no advertising and no tracking, and no information is ever sold.

Who uses the app

Only tournament staff use the app: admins, tournament directors, court captains and treasurers. Players do not install it and have no account. Players' information reaches the app from their tournament registration.

The camera is used only when a court captain chooses to photograph a score sheet. The app does not use location, contacts, or any other information on the phone.

What is public

The live scores page and the published past results show players by first name and last initial (for example "Pat L."), with their scores and standings. They never show email addresses, phone numbers or DUPR IDs. Anyone with the link can see these pages.

How it is protected

All information sent between the app and the club's server is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in codes and sessions are stored scrambled (hashed), never as plain text. Only staff with the right role can see players' contact details. Android may include the app's tournaments in the phone owner's own Google backup.

How long it is kept

Tournament information is kept so the club has a record of its events, until the club deletes it. A director can delete a tournament from their phone at any time. Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes, and sessions after 90 days.

Deleting your information

  • Staff accounts: in the app, Account → Delete my account, or on the web at pickleball-pools.tbor.workers.dev/account/delete. This removes your profile, roles, court captain places and sessions. Scores you entered and the tournament history remain part of the tournament's record.
  • Players: ask the tournament director or the club to remove your information from a tournament or from the published results.

Children

The app is for adult tournament staff and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new date. Questions or requests: contact the Heart of Wisconsin Pickleball Association, or open an issue at github.com/tborwi/pickleball-pools.
